    Know the machines

    Sentry — rifle-armed biped. The head is a weak point for double damage.

    Stalker — fast quadruped that pounces. Back-pedal and shoot, or slide past it.

    Hornet — flying drone. Fragile, but it strafes. Short bursts work best.

    Juggernaut — heavy with a rocket pod. Its red laser marks the target. Flank it and hit the glowing back vent.

    Whiteout: the Rime

    Thrall: a crewman with crystal through him. Throws ice shards; the head is the weak point. Skitter: six-legged crystal crawler that pounces. Frost Drone: a crusted survey drone (removed by No drones). Colossus: ice golem that lobs shard clusters; hit the glowing heart-shard on its back. Bloom: a crystal nest that breeds Skitters; shoot the core, explosives work best.

    Cold: in the storm your warmth drains outdoors. Stand near a lit heat beacon or go indoors to warm up; at zero you freeze. Crevasses are fatal. Cross on the rope bridges or the pipeline.

    Dust Off: the Story Campaign

    Eight missions, each with its own kit and a briefing before it. Militia carry rifles; machine gunners soak up more fire; RPG gunners shout before they fire, so move when you hear it. A sniper's red laser settles on you before the shot. Technicals shrug off rifle rounds: use explosives, or kill the gunner on the back. Your squad fights alongside you and the prisoners follow your path: don't leave them behind.

    Ghost Protocol is a stealth mission. The dark hides you, lamps and torch beams give you away, crouching keeps you quiet. Your weapons are suppressed; a melee attack from behind is a silent takedown. Guards who spot you raise the alarm after a few seconds unless you silence them, and a guard who finds a body comes looking. Never raising the alarm earns the Ghost bonus.

    Earning your kit

    Both campaigns start you with the M7 carbine and P-11 sidearm, and every objective releases more. In Cinder Foundry, clearing the yard unlocks the KS-12 shotgun, the first breaker unlocks satchel charges, restoring power unlocks the VX-3 rail, the halfway point of the upload unlocks the Rotor-6 minigun, and finishing the upload unlocks the Havoc RPG. Whiteout has its own order, ending with the Havoc RPG before the Hollow. Pause to see what's next.

    Kill streak — get 5 kills within 30 seconds to earn an attack drone. It fires 60 rounds in short bursts, then heads home. Turn on No drones to take the enemy Hornets out of the campaign; your kill-streak drone still works.

    Red barrels explode. Your health recovers when you avoid damage for a few seconds. Armor plates do not recover.

    Multiplayer loadouts

    Assault — M7 carbine. Breacher — KS-12 shotgun and 50 armor. Marksman — VX-3 rail, one-shot headshots. Gunner — HX-9 Warden LMG (100-round box, slow reload) and 25 armor. Heavy — Rotor-6 minigun (spins up before firing) and 50 armor. Demolition — Havoc RPG and satchel charges. Pyro — Ember-9 flamethrower (11 m of fire that passes through a crowd; anyone it touches burns for a few seconds more) and 25 armor. Every class starts with the P-11 sidearm and grenades. Armor and ammo pickups come back 20 seconds after they're taken.

    Edit any class in the lobby: pick its primary (any of the eight guns above, including the KF-44 revolver), its secondary, its equipment (two frags; satchel charges and a frag; or a bandolier of one frag and half again as much spare ammo) and its vest (none and a little faster, light with 25 armor, or heavy with 50 armor and a little slower), and rename it. Edits are saved in this browser; Reset class puts it back. Sniper Valley, Revolver One-Shot and co-op keep their fixed kits.

    Secondaries: P-11 Hollow (endless spare magazines), KF-44 Kingfisher revolver, VZ-9 Wasp machine pistol (fully automatic, 24 rounds, best inside 10 m), Hammer .50 hand cannon (two body shots, or one to an unarmored head), TR-3 Tempo (three-round bursts; two bursts kill), Mule Sawn-off (two shells, brutal in a room), Volt Arc Pistol (16 m of lightning that jumps through up to three people) and Pip-40 grenade pistol (one lobbed round that drops over distance and bursts on contact).

    Revolver One-Shot and the RC-XD

    Zombies shop: hits, kills and revives earn points. During a break press B to open the shop, then number keys buy max ammo, armor, weapons (the KF-44 revolver among them) and upgrades for the gun you're holding (Mk II to Mk IV).

    Revolver One-Shot: everyone carries the KF-44 revolver (6 rounds, 2.5 s reload) and any hit kills. Aim down sights; hip fire wanders. You're shielded for 2.5 s after spawning or until you fire. RC-XD (Nuketown): hold E at the chest between the bus and the truck, then press T to drive the car. Your body stands shielded while you drive. It explodes when you click or press T, after 20 seconds, when it crashes hard, or when enemies shoot it. The chest restocks 75 seconds after the car is gone.

    Coins, crates and saves

    Clearing campaign parts earns coins (40 each on Veteran; Recruit 75%, Elite 150%) and finishing a campaign pays a bonus. Spend them on crates in the Locker & Shop for weapon finishes and operative suits; duplicates refund coins. Taking #1 on a campaign leaderboard with at least 5 players unlocks the Champion gear, and while you hold #1 a crown halo and trail follow you in multiplayer. Campaign progress saves at every checkpoint; your save code carries everything to another device.
