Know the machines

Sentry — rifle-armed biped. The head is a weak point for double damage.

Stalker — fast quadruped that pounces. Back-pedal and shoot, or slide past it.

Hornet — flying drone. Fragile, but it strafes. Short bursts work best.

Juggernaut — heavy with a rocket pod. Its red laser marks the target. Flank it and hit the glowing back vent.

Red barrels explode. Your health recovers when you avoid damage for a few seconds. Armor plates do not recover.
